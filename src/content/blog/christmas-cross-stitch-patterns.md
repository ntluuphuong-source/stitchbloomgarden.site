Christmas is about nine weeks away, and that is exactly the right time to pick a holiday pattern. A small ornament takes an evening or two, but a framed piece you want to give as a gift usually needs three to six weeks of relaxed stitching. Start now and you will be finishing with a cup of cocoa, not stitching in a panic on Christmas Eve.

We grouped our favorite Christmas patterns from both of our shops by mood, so you can jump to the kind of Christmas you like to stitch. Every pattern is an instant PDF download with a color chart, a symbol chart and a DMC thread list.

## How to finish in time

- **Pick by size.** As a rough guide, a small motif under 50 x 50 stitches takes an evening or two. A framed piece around 100 x 100 stitches takes most people three to five weeks at an hour a day.
- **Use bigger fabric if you are short on time.** Stitching on 14 count Aida is faster to count than 18 count, and the finished piece is bigger. Our [Aida count guide](/blog/aida-count-finished-size/) shows how to work out the final size.
- **Stitch the gifts first.** Finish what you will give away by mid-December, then stitch something for yourself over the holidays.

Not sure where to start? Our [beginner's guide to cross stitch](/blog/beginner-cross-stitch-guide/) covers supplies and your first stitches. Happy holiday stitching!
