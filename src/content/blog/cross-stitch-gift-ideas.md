Cross stitch makes a lovely gift, because it is handmade, personal and not something you can grab from a shelf. The trick is knowing which kind of gift fits which person. A friend who already stitches wants something different from a cousin who has never held a needle, and both want something different from a coworker who just likes funny things on the wall.

This guide sorts ideas by who you are shopping for, with pattern picks you can stitch yourself or hand to a stitcher friend as an instant-download gift.

## Three ways to give cross stitch

**1. Give a finished piece.** You stitch it, frame it, and gift it. This works for everyone, stitcher or not, and small designs (a bookmark, a mini hoop, an ornament) can be finished in an evening or a few weekends. If you are planning for the holidays, our guide to [how long cross stitch takes](/blog/how-long-does-cross-stitch-take/) will help you work backwards from the date.

**2. Give a pattern to a stitcher.** Digital patterns are ideal for last-minute gifts. Send the PDF by email, add a note, and pair it with a little thread, a needle case or a bit of fabric if you have time to wrap something.

**3. Give a stitching starter set to a curious beginner.** A hoop, a few skeins, some Aida and a simple pattern is all it takes. Point them to our [beginner cross stitch guide](/blog/beginner-cross-stitch-guide/) so they know where to start.

## Tips for gifting your stitching

- **Choose a small size first.** If you have not stitched much, pick a pattern you can finish comfortably before the date.
- **Frame it.** A basic frame or hoop makes even a simple piece look like a finished gift. Our post on [finishing a cross stitch ornament](/blog/how-to-finish-a-cross-stitch-ornament/) shows one way to finish small pieces neatly.
- **Add a note.** A short line on the back or in the card about why you chose that design makes it feel personal.
- **Check the count.** If you are buying fabric too, our [Aida count guide](/blog/aida-count-finished-size/) shows how to work out the finished size.
