## Tips for stitching cats

**Choose the right fabric color.** Black cats show up best on light fabric such as white or cream Aida. If you prefer a dark background, check the pattern notes first. Our [Aida count guide](/blog/aida-count-finished-size/) helps you work out the finished size.

**Stitch dark areas in good light.** Black and dark gray thread is harder to see than other colors, so work near a window or lamp and take breaks.

**Use backstitch for whiskers and faces.** Fine details like whiskers, eyes and outlines are usually added with backstitch at the end. If you are not sure what the lines on a chart mean, our guide to [reading a cross stitch chart](/blog/how-to-read-a-cross-stitch-chart/) explains them.

**Keep the stitch direction consistent.** Make every top stitch face the same way so dark areas look smooth.

**Start small.** If this is your first cat pattern, try a design with a few colors. Our post on [how long cross stitch takes](/blog/how-long-does-cross-stitch-take/) will help you estimate time.
